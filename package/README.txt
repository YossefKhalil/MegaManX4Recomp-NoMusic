Mega Man X4 - No Music 1.0.1
============================

Author: Yossef Khalil
https://github.com/YossefKhalil

Built with OpenCode (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash).

Purpose
-------
Music-free play for players who prefer no music. I'm a Muslim and
prefer to play without music.

Behavior
--------
Silences XA/BGM1_U.XA, BGM2.XA, BGM3.XA, BGM4.XA and BGM5_U.XA via
disc_raw overlays. The stock BIN/CUE is not modified.

These are the only music streams referenced by the executable, so
title, menu, stage and boss music are muted.

Voices (XA/VOICE*), boss intros (XA/BOSINT), cutscene audio
(STR/*.STR) and SPU sound effects are unchanged.

Usage
-----
Enable "Mute All Music (Menus + Gameplay)" on the Mods page, select
the original Mega Man X4 (USA).cue, and boot fresh.
